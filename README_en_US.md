# Quantify Your Time & Review with Clarity — "Time Spent" Plugin for SiYuan

## 💡 Why Do You Need "Time Spent"?

When using SiYuan Note for **academic research, competitive exam prep, thematic writing, professional learning, or project management**, do you frequently encounter these frustrations:

- 🕒 **Busy all day, yet wondering where the time went**: Opening and closing notes continuously, only to feel exhausted by nightfall with vague recollections and no tangible sense of achievement.
- 🛑 **Traditional timer tools break your deep flow state**: Whether using Pomodoro timers or manual stopwatches, you are forced to constantly click "Start / Stop". Forgetting to start or stop ruins the record, and managing the timer itself becomes a distraction.
- 💦 **Idle time artificially inflates your stats**: Walking away for coffee, picking up your phone, or daydreaming while the timer blindly counts upwards produces fake "8 hours of focus" that misleads your self-assessment.
- 📉 **Periodic reviews lack objective data support**: At the end of the week or month, you want to optimize your working rhythm, but you lack clear visibility into your actual time investment and time sinks across topics and documents.

**"Time Spent" is engineered specifically to solve these challenges** — deeply integrated into SiYuan Note, it silently and automatically captures your genuine focus trajectory across every document, intelligently removes idle time, and provides vibrant Day / Week / Month dashboards along with an AI productivity coach to help you master deep work and learning rhythms.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🔄 Automatic, Frictionless Tracking

- **Track on Open**: Open any note or switch documents, and the plugin automatically transitions records in the background without manual intervention.
- **Zero Flow Disruption**: Stay completely immersed in thinking, reading, and writing without wasting cognitive energy on tracking time.

### 2. 🛡️ Intelligent Anti-Idle Filtering

- **Millisecond Peripheral Sensing**: Monitors keystrokes, mouse movement, scroll wheel actions, and touch events in real time.
- **Accurate Idle Deduction**: When prolonged inactivity exceeds your configured threshold, that duration is marked as idle and cleanly deducted, ensuring your stats reflect 100% genuine focus.

### 3. 🎯 Focus Goal Setting with Dynamic Presets

- **Periodic Goal Management**: Set daily, weekly, or monthly focus pledges (e.g., *"Complete Chapter 4 notes, focus for 4 hours"*).
- **Contextual Suggestions**: Intelligently recommends goal candidate phrases based on the active calendar scope (Day / Week / Month).
- **End-to-End Integration**: Goals stay pinned on the Hero card and serve as the core benchmark during AI reviews.

### 4. 📅 Day / Week / Month Panoramic Dashboards

| View Type | Core Display | Best Suited For |
| :-------- | :----------- | :-------------- |
| **Day View** | **24-hour vertical time-blocking canvas** + **live current-time red indicator** + **detailed activity session log** | Inspecting daily time allocation and chronotype rhythms, retracing specific focus blocks and durations. |
| **Week View** | **7-day horizontal bar comparison** + **daily investment trends** + **one-click header drill-down** | Evaluating weekday vs. weekend energy variance and locating your peak productive hours. |
| **Month View** | **Full month calendar heatmap matrix** + **daily top-document focus badges** + **monthly focus trend curve** | Macro insights on month-long consistency and habit formation, reviewing major projects per phase. |

### 5. 📊 5 Key Personal Efficiency KPIs + Interactive Charts

The dashboard summarizes 5 critical personal efficiency metrics in real time:

1. **Total Focus Duration**: Net effective study and work time after idle deductions.
2. **Daily Average / Peak Focus**: Measuring sustained capacity and peak hour/day output.
3. **Session Count & Average Duration**: Evaluating focus depth to detect fragmented task-switching.
4. **Idle Deducted Time**: Clear visibility into inactive time filtered by the system.
5. **Core Priority Ratio**: Intuitive breakdown of top documents by time and percentage.

**Interactive Visualizations**:

- **Document / Notebook / Tag Distribution Donut Chart**: Multi-dimensional breakdown of time invested.
- **Time Trend & Comparison Charts**: 24-hour distribution bar chart, weekly comparison, and monthly trend line.

## 🚀 Quick Start

### 1. Automatic Background Tracking

- Upon installation and activation, tracking starts automatically in the background.
- Simply read, write, and think inside SiYuan as you normally do.

### 2. Open the Dashboard

- Click the **"Time Spent" clock icon** in SiYuan's top toolbar to launch the dashboard.
- Choose whether to open in a **new tab** or a **modal window** in the plugin settings.

### 3. Set Your Focus Goal

- Type your primary objective into the **Focus Goal** card at the top to direct your energy.

## ⚙️ FAQ

- **Will background tracking slow down SiYuan or cause typing lag?**  
  Not at all. Time Spent employs strict event throttling and lightweight state updates. Data writes occur minimally on note switching and window state changes, maintaining a tiny memory footprint.
- **What happens if I step away from my computer with a document open?**  
  The plugin continuously checks for input. When no keyboard, mouse, or touch events occur beyond your idle threshold, the period is classified as idle and subtracted when you return.
- **Where is my data stored? How do I migrate across devices?**  
  All records are saved as standard JSON files in your local workspace at `data/storage/petal/siyuan-time-spent/`. If you use SiYuan's official cloud sync or workspace backups, your records sync automatically to new devices.
- **How do I use AI Deep Review? Is an API key required?**  
  AI Deep Review offers two modes:
  1. **Direct Generation**: Provide any OpenAI-compatible API endpoint and key in plugin settings (or inherit via *API Switch* plugin) to stream reviews directly.
  2. **Copy Mode**: Even without API credentials, clicking "Copy Summary" lets you paste formatted statistics and tuned prompts into ChatGPT, Claude, Kimi, or any external assistant.
